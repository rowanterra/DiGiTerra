F1 (established). DiGiTerra rounds regression metrics to three decimals
before the significant-figures setting is applied. With sigfig 10 the page
and the manifest give test RMSE "53.8080000000" and R2 "0.5200000000" for
values of 53.808429803715498 and 0.51950439781862778
(digiterra_outputs/c000_base_a/result.json, manifest.json metrics vs
metrics_from_predictions.csv). Cause: python_scripts/preprocessing/
run_regression_pipeline.py lines 669 to 670 call regression_report() without
sigfig, so its default 3 applies; python_scripts/app_model_training.py lines
887 to 894 then format the rounded value with sigfig decimals. The setting is
decimals, not significant figures (fmt ".{sigfig}f", line 677). The paper says
the manifest carries the metrics; it carries them rounded and as strings.

(python_scripts/app_exploration.py line 261, .round(4)); the diabetes column
means print as -0.0 and 0.0 (digiterra_outputs/D1/D1_digiterra.csv). Every
D1 and D2 cell equals the oracle rounded to four decimals (67 cells), so the
statistics are right and the display is coarse.
